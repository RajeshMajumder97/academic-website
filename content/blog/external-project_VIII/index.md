summary: In this tutorial, we move from the basic biology of DNA, nucleosomes, promoters, enhancers, and transcription factors to the practical analysis of bulk ATAC-seq data. Using two human cell states—IMR90 differentiated fibroblast-like cells and GM23338 iPSC-like cells—we explore how chromatin accessibility differs between cellular states, from sequencing quality control and peak detection to consensus peaks, TSS annotation, motif enrichment, and nearest-gene mapping.
The goal is not only to understand what ATAC-seq measures, but also to develop an intuitive understanding of how accessibility patterns reveal the regulatory architecture underlying cell identity.
